Quiet Observation. Authentic Direction.

My approach to photography is rooted in honesty. I don't believe in forcing awkward poses or manufacturing moments. Instead, I observe quietly, directing only when necessary to find the best light and the most genuine interaction.

The result is a collection of images that feels cinematic, timeless, and completely true to how it actually felt to be there.

The Photographer

Hello, I'm Sarah.

I am a fine art and documentary photographer based in the city, but travelling wherever stories take me. I've spent the last decade documenting celebrations, quiet elopements, and the beautiful, messy reality of human connection.

When I'm not behind the camera, you can usually find me hunting for vintage furniture, drinking too much black coffee, or exploring the coast.
