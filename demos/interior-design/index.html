The Process

01

Discover

Initial consultation to understand the brief, lifestyle requirements, and aesthetic vision.

02

Concept

Developing spatial layouts and establishing the preliminary material and colour palette.

03

Design

Refining details, selecting specific finishes, fixtures, and producing technical drawings.

04

Detail

Tendering to contractors, finalising budgets, and preparing for construction.

05

Execute

Site management, procurement, installation, and final styling of the completed space.
