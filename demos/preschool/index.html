Safety Is Our Baseline.

As parents ourselves, we know that nothing matters more than your child's physical and emotional safety. Our premises are designed to be secure, hygienic, and constantly supervised.

  • Secure Premises

    CCTV monitoring, child-safe entry systems, and strict pick-up protocols.

  • Child-Safe Infrastructure

    Rounded furniture edges, finger-pinch guards on doors, and soft-fall surfaces in play areas.

  • Trained Educators

    All staff undergo background checks and are trained in basic pediatric First Aid and CPR.

Questions Parents Ask

We maintain strict ratios to ensure individual attention. Playgroup is 1:6, Nursery is 1:8, and Kindergarten is 1:10, always supported by dedicated support staff.
For our daycare children, we provide fresh, nutritious, child-friendly vegetarian meals prepared in our hygienic in-house kitchen. Preschool children are required to bring a healthy snack box from home.
We follow a gentle, phased settling-in process. Parents are welcome to stay in the waiting area for the first few days while the child builds a bond with their primary educator. We never force a crying child to stay without comfort.
